Starting to code with no experience has been so hard for me. Everything feels completely new, and I’ve struggled to understand even the simplest concepts. Words like “syntax,” “loops,” and “variables” were confusing at first, and figuring out what they mean has taken a lot of effort. On top of that, there’s so much to learn at once, and even writing a small piece of code can take me hours. Sometimes, just a missing semicolon or a small mistake makes the whole thing stop working, and I don’t even know where to start fixing it.
The error messages are one of the worst parts. They seem so technical and complicated, and I often have no idea what they’re trying to tell me. It’s frustrating to feel stuck on something that seems so small. I’ve also found it hard to feel like I’m making any real progress. Coding doesn’t always give you instant results, so sometimes I wonder if I’m even improving.
Another thing that’s been tough is figuring out what resources to use. There are so many tutorials and videos out there, but some of them are hard to follow, and I don’t know which ones are actually helpful. On top of that, seeing other people pick it up so quickly makes me doubt myself sometimes. But even though it’s been frustrating, I keep reminding myself that everyone starts somewhere, and little by little, I’ll get better.
